About MultiWeb

Free online tools built with care for speed, privacy, and simplicity.

Our Mission

MultiWeb was created with a simple goal: provide high-quality, free online tools that work instantly in your browser — no sign-ups, no clutter, no data collection. We believe everyday tasks like converting documents, calculating finances, or generating passwords should be quick, accurate, and accessible to everyone, everywhere.

Every tool on MultiWeb is built with modern web standards, runs entirely in your browser for maximum privacy, and is designed to work beautifully on both desktop and mobile devices. We prioritize speed, accuracy, and a clean user experience — because your time matters.

What Makes MultiWeb Different

  • 100% Free — No sign-ups, no subscriptions, no hidden fees. Every tool is completely free to use.
  • Privacy by Design — Your data never leaves your browser. No servers, no uploads, no tracking of your inputs.
  • Works Offline — Once loaded, most tools work without an internet connection thanks to our service worker.
  • Mobile-First — Responsive design that works beautifully on phones, tablets, and desktops.
  • Multilingual — Full support for 11 languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi, and Arabic.
  • No Account Required — Jump in, use a tool, and leave. No emails, no passwords, no strings attached.

How It Works

MultiWeb is a static website — just HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. There are no servers, no databases, and no backend. Here's what happens when you use a tool:

  • Your browser downloads the page and its scripts once
  • All processing happens locally using your device's CPU
  • No data is sent to any server during tool usage
  • When you close the page, all data is gone — nothing persists

This architecture means your files, calculations, and inputs never leave your device. It's privacy by design, not by policy.

Technology

MultiWeb is built with modern, lightweight web technologies:

  • HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript — No frameworks, no build tools, no bloat
  • Google Fonts (Inter) — Clean, readable typography
  • Service Worker — Enables offline caching and fast repeat visits
  • CSS Custom Properties — Theming (light/dark mode) without JavaScript overhead
  • Open Source Libraries — mammoth.js, pdf.js, SheetJS, QRious, jsPDF, and pdfmake for document and QR processing
  • JSON-LD Structured Data — SEO-friendly markup for search engines
  • Cloudflare Pages — Fast, global CDN hosting with automatic SSL

Privacy First

We take privacy seriously. None of our tools send your data to external servers. Everything runs locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your inputs, calculations, and files never leave your device.

The only cookies used are for analytics (Google Analytics) and advertising (Google AdSense), and only after you explicitly consent via our cookie banner. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
