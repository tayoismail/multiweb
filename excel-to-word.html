Excel to Word Converter — Turn Spreadsheets into Word Tables

Need your spreadsheet data in a Word document? Whether you're writing a report, a proposal, or an invoice that includes tabular data, our free Excel to Word converter turns your .xlsx spreadsheet into a Word document with a proper, fully editable table — sheet by sheet.


Like every tool on MultiWeb, the conversion runs entirely in your browser. Your spreadsheet is never uploaded to a server, so budgets, client data, and other sensitive figures stay completely private.


Every sheet in your workbook becomes a section in the Word document, with the sheet name as a heading and the data as a bordered table. The first row is formatted as a header row so your document reads cleanly. Going the other way? Try our Word to Excel converter.

How to Convert Excel to Word

  1. Upload your Excel file Drag and drop your .xlsx file onto the converter, or click the drop zone to browse your device.
  2. Click "Convert to Word" The converter reads every sheet and rebuilds your data as Word tables — all within your browser.
  3. Download and edit Your .docx file downloads automatically. Open it in Word, Google Docs, or LibreOffice and edit freely.
