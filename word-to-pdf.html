Word to PDF Converter — Convert DOCX to PDF Free

Converting a Word document to PDF is one of the most common document tasks there is — whether you're sending a résumé, submitting a report, or sharing a contract. PDFs preserve your layout, fonts, and formatting on any device, which makes them the standard for professional and official documents. Our free Word to PDF converter turns your .docx files into clean, print-ready PDFs in seconds.


Unlike most online converters, MultiWeb processes your document entirely in your browser. Your file is never uploaded to a server, never stored, and never seen by anyone else. This makes it ideal for sensitive documents like contracts, invoices, and personal records. There are no file limits, no sign-ups, and no watermarks — just fast, private conversion.


The converter supports Word documents created by Microsoft Word, Google Docs, and LibreOffice (.docx format). Text, headings, lists, tables, and images are preserved in the output PDF. If you need to go the other direction, try our PDF to Word converter.

How to Convert Word to PDF

  2. Click "Convert to PDF" The converter reads your document and rebuilds it as a PDF — all within your browser, so nothing is uploaded.
