Word to Excel Converter — Turn Word Tables into Spreadsheets

Need the data from a Word document in Excel? Budgets, invoices, price lists, and reports are often written in Word with tables — but analyzing, sorting, and recalculating that data is much easier in a spreadsheet. Our free Word to Excel converter extracts the tables from your .docx file and rebuilds them as a real Excel workbook, cell by cell.


Like every tool on MultiWeb, the conversion runs entirely in your browser. Your document is never uploaded to a server, which keeps sensitive data — quotes, client lists, financial figures — completely private.


Each table in your Word document becomes its own sheet in the Excel workbook (Table 1, Table 2, …), and any surrounding text is saved to a "Text" sheet so nothing is lost. If the document has no tables, the text is placed as rows. Going the other way? Try our Excel to Word converter.

How to Convert Word to Excel

  1. Upload your Word document Drag and drop your .docx file onto the converter, or click the drop zone to browse your device.
  2. Click "Convert to Excel" The converter finds every table in your document and builds an Excel workbook — all within your browser.
  3. Download and analyze Your .xlsx file downloads automatically. Open it in Excel, Google Sheets, or LibreOffice and work with the data freely.
