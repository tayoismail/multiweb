PDF to Excel Converter — Extract Data from PDFs

PDFs lock your data in. When you need to work with figures from a report, invoice, or statement in a spreadsheet, our free PDF to Excel converter extracts the text and table-like data from your PDF and rebuilds it as an .xlsx workbook — row by row, cell by cell.


Like every tool on MultiWeb, the conversion runs entirely in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to a server, making this converter safe for bank statements, contracts, and other private documents.


Please note: PDF files store text without table structure, so column boundaries are detected automatically and may need minor cleanup for very complex layouts. For documents without selectable text (for example, scanned pages), nothing can be extracted — we'll let you know if that's the case. Going the other way? Try our Excel to PDF converter.

How to Convert PDF to Excel

  1. Upload your PDF Drag and drop your PDF file onto the converter, or click the drop zone to browse your device.
  2. Click "Convert to Excel" The converter reads every page, groups the text into rows, and detects column breaks — all within your browser.
  3. Download and refine Your .xlsx file downloads automatically. Open it in Excel or Google Sheets and tidy up any rows as needed.
