========================================
  MultiWeb — Free Online Utility Tools
  https://multiwebs.app/
========================================

MultiWeb is a free collection of 32+ online tools, calculators, converters, and generators — all running entirely in your browser with zero sign-ups required.

========================================
  WHAT IT DOES
========================================

MultiWeb provides instant, private, browser-based tools for everyday tasks. No files are uploaded to any server; everything is processed locally on your device using JavaScript.

========================================
  TOOLS INCLUDED
========================================

--- Document Converters ---
  Word to PDF Converter      — Convert Word (.docx) files to PDF in your browser.
  PDF to Word Converter      — Convert PDF files back to editable Word documents.
  Word to Excel Converter    — Extract Word tables into Excel spreadsheets.
  Excel to Word Converter    — Turn spreadsheets into Word documents with tables.
  PDF to Excel Converter     — Extract text and tables from PDF into Excel.
  Excel to PDF Converter     — Turn spreadsheets into clean, print-ready PDFs.

--- Calculators ---
  BMI Calculator             — Calculate Body Mass Index from height and weight.
  Percentage Calculator      — Find percentages, discounts, tips, and proportions.
  Tip Calculator             — Calculate tips and split restaurant bills.
  Discount Calculator        — Calculate sale price and savings after any discount.
  Sales Tax Calculator       — Add sales tax to purchases and know the total cost.
  Unit Converter             — Convert length, weight, temperature, volume, and more.

--- Finance & Money ---
  Mortgage Calculator        — Calculate monthly payments, total interest, amortization.
  Currency Converter         — Convert between 150+ world currencies instantly.
  ROI Calculator             — Calculate return on investment for any project.
  Credit Card Payoff Calculator — Find how long to pay off credit card debt.
  SIP Returns Calculator     — Calculate returns on monthly mutual fund investments.
  Investment Growth Calculator — See how investments grow with compound interest.
  Retirement Calculator      — Plan your retirement corpus based on age and expenses.
  Budget Calculator          — Plan your monthly budget and track income vs expenses.
  Net Worth Calculator       — Calculate total net worth (assets minus liabilities).
  Savings Goal Calculator    — Find how much to save monthly to reach your goal.
  Inflation Calculator       — See how inflation affects purchasing power over time.
  Markup Calculator          — Calculate markup percentage and set selling prices.
  Profit Margin Calculator   — Calculate profit margin and gross profit for business.
  Break-Even Calculator      — Find how many units to sell to cover all costs.

--- Generators & Utilities ---
  QR Code Generator          — Create QR codes for URLs, text, Wi-Fi, and contacts.
  Password Generator         — Generate strong, random passwords with custom settings.
  Word Counter               — Count words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs.
  Word Unscrambler           — Unscramble letters for Scrabble, crosswords, and games.
  Image Converter            — Convert images between PNG, JPG, and WebP formats.
  OG Image Generator         — Create custom Open Graph images for social media sharing.

========================================
  KEY FEATURES
========================================

  * 100% Free — No sign-ups, no hidden fees.
  * Private — All processing happens in your browser. Files never leave your device.
  * Works Offline — Most tools work without an internet connection.
  * Mobile-Friendly — Responsive design works beautifully on phones and tablets.
  * 11 Languages — Full internationalization (i18n) support.
  * Dark Mode — Toggle between light and dark themes.
  * Search & Filter — Quickly find tools by category or keyword.

========================================
  TECHNOLOGY
========================================

  * HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript (no frameworks)
  * Google Fonts (Inter) — the only external dependency
  * Open Graph meta tags for social sharing
  * JSON-LD structured data for SEO
  * Service Worker (sw.js) for offline support
  * Responsive CSS with CSS custom properties

  PROJECT FILES
========================================

  common-styles.css         — Shared CSS styles across all pages
  common.js                 — Shared JavaScript (theme toggle, nav, etc.)

  i18n.css                  — i18n-related styles
  i18n/                     — Translation files (one per language)
  converter.js              — Document conversion utilities
  sw.js                     — Service worker for offline caching

  og-default.png            — Default Open Graph image
  robots.txt                — Search engine crawl rules
  sitemap.xml               — XML sitemap for SEO
  ads.txt                   — Ad transparency file

========================================
  HOW TO USE
========================================

  1. Open index.html in any modern browser (or visit the live site).
  2. Click a tool card or use the search bar to find what you need.
  3. Enter your data — results appear instantly.
  4. No account, no upload, no waiting.
