PDF to Word Converter — Turn PDFs into Editable Documents

PDFs are great for sharing, but editing them is a pain. When you need to change a few words, fix a typo, or reuse the content of a PDF, converting it to a Word document is the fastest path. Our free PDF to Word converter extracts the text from your PDF and builds a fully editable .docx file — no sign-up, no watermarks, and no file limits.


Like every tool on MultiWeb, the conversion runs entirely in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to a server, which makes this converter safe for contracts, bank statements, and other documents you'd rather keep private.


Text and basic structure are preserved, so you can edit, reformat, and reuse your content in Word. Please note that very complex PDF layouts — such as multi-column pages or heavy graphics — are simplified during conversion. For documents without embedded selectable text (for example, scanned pages), no text can be extracted; we'll let you know if that's the case. Going the other way? Try our Word to PDF converter.

How to Convert PDF to Word

  1. Upload your PDF Drag and drop your PDF file onto the converter, or click the drop zone to browse your device.
  2. Click "Convert to Word" The converter reads every page of your PDF and rebuilds the text as a Word document — all within your browser.
  3. Download and edit Your .docx file downloads automatically. Open it in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or LibreOffice and edit freely.
