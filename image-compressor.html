Image Compressor — Reduce Image File Size Without Losing Quality

An image compressor reduces the file size of your photos and graphics so they load faster on websites, take less storage space, and are easier to share via email or messaging apps. Our free online compressor uses advanced algorithms to shrink file sizes while preserving visual quality.


Unlike tools that upload your images to remote servers, this compressor runs entirely in your browser. Your images are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted anywhere. This makes it completely private and secure — ideal for sensitive photos, documents, or personal images.


You can compress multiple images at once (batch mode), choose an output format (JPG, WebP, or PNG), set a quality level, resize to maximum dimensions, or even specify a target file size in KB. A side-by-side preview shows you exactly how much space you've saved.

How to Compress an Image

  1. Upload your image(s) Drag and drop one or more images onto the upload area, or click to browse. We support PNG, JPG, WebP, BMP, and GIF formats.
  2. Choose your settings Select an output format (or keep original), adjust the quality slider, optionally set max dimensions or a target file size in KB.
  3. Compress Click the "Compress" button. Compression happens instantly in your browser. A preview shows the original vs compressed versions with file sizes.
  4. Download Review the results and click "Download" to save your compressed image(s). For batch mode, all files download as a summary.
